    How to use this guide

    1. Play the story first — chapters 1 to 4 and the finale earn 12 trophies.
    2. Clear the missable ones before the finale: partner trophies, one-off stunts and story choices cannot be replayed except from mission replay.
    3. Tick each trophy as you earn it; progress is saved in this browser.
    4. Export your progress to a file before clearing browser data, and import it back any time.

    Names, descriptions, grades and counts are the official PlayStation list as published via Exophase and PSNProfiles. Guides are written in our own words for this entry and cite their sources beside each trophy. No Rockstar artwork is used.
