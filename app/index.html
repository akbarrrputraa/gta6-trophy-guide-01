    Roadmap to the Platinum — “Vice Got a Price!”

    About 200 hours, difficulty roughly 4/10. There is no mission replay and no New Game Plus, so keep a manual save at the start of every chapter and one before the final mission. Cheats disable trophies and saving, and only unlock after the story anyway.

      Before you start

      • One possibly missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude. Meditate at Lucia’s balcony spot in Chapter 1 before the chapter ends — it disappears after that.
      • No online trophies: all 55 are single-player and offline.
      • 100% completion: the A Long Road gold uses the in-game checklist (Progress > Stats > 100% Completion). It appears after the story, but earlier progress counts.
